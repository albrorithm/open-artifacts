# Reading comment targets

Read each thread's original `revision_id`, `review_target`, and captured controls before interpreting a note. `review_target` is decoded evidence of what the reviewer selected. The unchanged `view_state_json` also keeps the serialized `__oa_target` and any `__oa_point` placement. `is_from_earlier_revision` is true when the original `revision_id` differs from `artifact.current_revision_id`, including when reading historical HTML. This flag is derived on reads, not stored as target evidence or a live layout check.

| Kind | What it identifies |
| --- | --- |
| `element` | An HTML element. Use its `tag`, normalized `text`, accessible `label`, and available paths or `reviewId` to identify the subject. A labeled icon button can have no visible text. |
| `text` | The visible selected quote in `text.exact`, including leading or trailing whitespace, partial words, and paragraph breaks. `prefix` and `suffix` give nearby DOM text. `startOffset` and `endOffset` are UTF-16 positions in the containing element's `textContent`; they distinguish repeated quotes and can span inline elements or paragraphs. |
| `point` | A position in the artifact, with surrounding element evidence when available. Read coordinates from `__oa_point`; do not claim that the reviewer selected a particular word or control. |
| `area` | A rectangular region. `area.rect` uses CSS pixels relative to the captured containing element. `area.viewport` and `area.container` preserve dimensions; `area.members` contains up to eight covered elements in page order, each with its own `element`, rectangle and `coverage` of `full` or `partial`. `area.truncated` marks incomplete coverage evidence. |

A CSS path is supporting evidence when present, not a required field for every target. Text and area targets include a containing element; point targets may have none. Legacy notes have `review_target: null` and retain their earlier excerpt, anchor, section, and captured state. Do not invent more precise targets for them.

Text selection can add visible paragraph breaks that do not appear in DOM text. When those strings differ, optional `text.domExact` holds the raw DOM range for matching against source offsets; `text.exact` remains the quote to show and discuss. For source matching use `domExact ?? exact`. Prefix, suffix, and offsets always describe DOM text. Older text targets without `domExact` remain valid.

Exact text selections can begin or end in the middle of a word. The UI's **Full selection** is the complete captured span, not an abbreviated sentence. A selection such as `quiet deta` can correctly match within `quiet detail`; surrounding characters are outside the selection. Compare the selected span and saved context, rather than requiring a whole-word or whole-sentence match.

An area's member text describes the intersected elements, not a precise selected quote. A `partial` member can include text outside the rectangle. Empty regions are valid and may contain no readable elements. An unlabeled graphic identifies a graphic, not its visual contents. Do not infer unseen contents or treat a truncated member list as complete. Captured context excludes editable values and script/style contents; metadata remains bounded to 20,000 characters. Areas do not include screenshots.

Targets belong to the original comment revision. Current HTML may have changed. Read that original revision when needed, then compare its subject with the current source. A later revision needs a unique stable anchor or unique exact captured-content match; a positional path alone can point to different content after an edit. Text matches retain the quote and surrounding context even if offsets move. Areas and element-relative points also need matching container dimensions and layout. A container can move without invalidating its region, but a resized or rearranged container cannot. Raw page-coordinate points remain on their original revision.

New area captures include `area.layout` with a bounded geometry fingerprint and a `truncated` flag. Element-relative points can carry a corresponding fingerprint in `geometry.layout`. These summarize element and text-line positions, so text wrapping or movement inside a fixed-size box also prevents placement. They are saved evidence for the browser's comparison, not images or a live match result. Older captures without complete layout evidence remain readable but cannot establish area or point placement on a later revision.

The browser previews the chosen target before submission. The document root and sole page wrappers cannot be picked as elements; blank-space drags can still capture areas there. With a mouse or trackpad, dragging blank space draws an area; dragging text keeps native text selection. Escape cancels the drag. Touch gestures keep normal scrolling; there is no explicit area-selection control. Saved area comments show **Selected area**, coverage and their original revision.

The displayed iframe separately reports placement as checking, matching, changed, missing, ambiguous, or not checked. Locate draws a pin, exact text range or rectangle only when placement matches. Unmatched comments keep their captured context and original-revision link in the list. Initial checking is not missing. If evidence conflicts or several elements fit, report the uncertainty rather than silently reattaching the note. MCP and WebMCP results expose captured evidence; they do not claim a live layout match.
